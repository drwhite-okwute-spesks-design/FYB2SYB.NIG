2. Early Life and Family Background

Alhaji Yahaya Adoza Bello was born on 18 June 1975 in Agassa, Okene Local Government Area of Kogi State, Nigeria. He grew up in Kogi State and began his formal education before proceeding to higher education.

His background in education, accounting and business preceded his entry into public service and Nigerian politics. He later became a prominent political figure in Kogi State through his election as governor in 2015 and assumption of office in 2016.

3. Educational Background

His reported educational history includes the following:

  • Primary education: LGEA Primary School, Agassa, Okene.
  • Secondary education: Government Secondary School, Suleja, Niger State.
  • University education: Ahmadu Bello University, where he studied Accounting.
  • Further studies: He is reported to have pursued a Master of Business Administration (MBA).
  • Professional training: His biography also refers to professional accounting qualifications.

4. Professional Career Before Politics

Before entering elective politics, Yahaya Bello worked in public service and business. His professional background included accounting, and his biography identifies experience connected with the Revenue Mobilization Allocation and Fiscal Commission during his National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) period.

He subsequently pursued private business interests before becoming involved in the political activities that led to his emergence as governor of Kogi State.

5. Political Career

Yahaya Bello rose to national prominence during the 2015 Kogi State governorship election. He was the All Progressives Congress candidate who eventually became governor following the death of the party's original candidate, Prince Abubakar Audu.

He assumed office as Governor of Kogi State on 27 January 2016. He was re-elected in the 2019 governorship election and served until 27 January 2024.

Ahmed Usman Ododo succeeded him as governor in January 2024.

6. His Administration in Kogi State

During his eight years as governor, his administration announced programmes and policies covering various areas of state governance.

  • Education and youth development.
  • Healthcare services.

  • Infrastructure and public works.

Yahaya Bello completed his second term as Governor of Kogi State in January 2024. He is a former governor and a member of the All Progressives Congress (APC).
